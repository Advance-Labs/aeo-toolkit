# Managed / Autopilot — 90-Day Guarantee Terms (DRAFT SKELETON)

> ⚠️ **RELEASE GATE — NOT LEGAL TEXT.** This is a structural skeleton flagged by the Autopilot
> check-agent review (spec §0.4). It MUST be reviewed and finalized by counsel before the Managed
> tier is marketed with any guarantee. Do not publish guarantee copy that promises a traffic or
> ranking *outcome* — that is the FTC/consumer-protection and breach-of-contract exposure the review
> flagged. Frame the guarantee as **work delivered**, not results achieved.

## 1. What is guaranteed (work-delivered SLA — NOT an outcome)
The guarantee is that, over the 90-day period, Advance Labs **delivers the agreed work**:
- up to N published articles / month (per the plan's `articlesPerMonth`),
- up to M compliant link-**outreach** placements pursued / month (per `outreachPlacementsPerMonth`),
- measurable AI-citation coverage tracking on the agreed target prompts.

**Remedy if the work is not delivered:** we continue working at no additional charge until the agreed
work is delivered (a "keep-working-free" service-credit remedy). **There is NO cash-back / outcome
guarantee** ("your traffic will increase or money back") — outcomes depend on Google/AI engines, which
we do not control.

## 2. Exclusions / what voids the guarantee (counsel to finalize)
- Customer inaction: failing to approve the work queue, blocking our crawler/agent, or not connecting
  the required integrations.
- Pre-existing penalties: a Google manual action or platform sanction on the customer's domain/accounts
  predating onboarding, or caused by the customer's other activity.
- Customer-directed changes that conflict with our recommendations.

## 3. Baseline & measurement
A visibility/citation baseline is captured at onboarding (`customer_profiles.guarantee_baseline`) so
delivery is measured objectively. The baseline records terms, target prompts, and the measurement
method in effect at the start of the period.

## 4. Claims process (counsel to define)
How a customer raises a claim, the review window, and how the keep-working-free remedy is administered.

## 5. Liability (counsel — see MSA)
Assumption-of-risk, limitation-of-liability, and indemnification live in `MSA-managed.md`. The guarantee
does not expand liability beyond those caps.
