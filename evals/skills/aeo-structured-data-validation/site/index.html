Reporting dashboards for small finance teams

Northwind Analytics builds reporting dashboards for finance teams of two to twenty people. We connect to QuickBooks and Xero, pull the ledger every night, and show cash, burn and receivables on one screen. Most teams are set up in a day.

Who it is for

Controllers and founders who close the books themselves and want month-end numbers without building spreadsheets by hand. If you already run a BI tool with a data team behind it, you probably do not need us.

What it costs

Plans start at 49 dollars a month for one ledger and go to 199 dollars a month for five ledgers with consolidated reporting.
