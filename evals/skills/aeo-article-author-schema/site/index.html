How to close the month in two days

By Priya Raman, Controller at Northwind Analytics. Published 2026-10-02, updated 2026-11-01.

A two-day close is realistic for a team of two to five people if you reconcile bank accounts daily rather than at month end. The remaining work is accruals, prepaid expenses and a review of receivables, and each of those has a checklist below.

What to reconcile first

Start with cash. Bank and card accounts that are reconciled daily leave nothing for the close except a final check that balances match the statements.

What to automate

Recurring journal entries for rent, payroll accruals and depreciation should post themselves. Review them, do not type them.
