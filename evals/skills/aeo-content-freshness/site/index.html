How to close the month in two days

By Priya Raman.

A two-day close is realistic for a team of two to five people if you reconcile bank accounts daily rather than at month end. The remaining work is accruals, prepaid expenses and a review of receivables.

What to reconcile first

Start with cash. Bank and card accounts that are reconciled daily leave nothing for the close except a final check that balances match the statements.
